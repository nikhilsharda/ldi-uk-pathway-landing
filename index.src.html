Straight answers

Requirements, in plain language.

Entry and potential progression conditions

Admission is assessed against the programme requirements. Potential progression to a UK university depends on meeting the relevant academic and English language requirements.

  • Your highest qualification and completion year
  • Your English language test status
  • Your preferred intake and study commitment
Exact requirements depend on your country and qualification. Share your details and a counsellor confirms them in one conversation.

People you can name

A counsellor guides you from enquiry to application, by video, phone or WhatsApp.

Fees, with no surprises

We share verified tuition and any applicable fees clearly before you apply. Send your country and programme interest to get the correct figures.

A learning format built around your week

The starting stage is delivered online, so you can begin from home. We share the schedule, learning commitment and potential progression details for your chosen programme before you apply.

Is admission guaranteed?

No. Admission is assessed against the programme requirements. Share your details and we will help you understand the eligibility steps.

Will I definitely progress to the UK?

Potential progression is subject to the relevant academic and English language requirements. We can explain the route and what you would need to achieve.

Is it fully online?

How much does it cost?

Can I work while studying?

That depends on your schedule and circumstances. We can explain the learning commitment so you can judge whether it fits your week.
